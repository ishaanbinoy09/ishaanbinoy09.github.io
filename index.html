About me

Freshman year, I got a C- on a biology test and stopped wanting to talk about school at all. I didn't fix that overnight. I moved to a school with a schedule that fit me better, went from eight classes to four at a time, and slowly my grades and my confidence came back.

Since then I've taught myself Python, built machine learning projects, refurbished laptops for families without them, and worked in a customer-facing job and a marketing internship at an AI company. Those experiences share one question that now drives me: when we build something, who is affected, and who gets left out?

Projects

Research

Bias in AI financial lending

A student research project on how machine learning models can treat borrowers unfairly, mentored by an AI graduate student through Inspirit AI. Our paper is in preparation.

What I found: The models showed real financial literacy, but once their advice was personalized to a borrower, bias crept in, even where none appeared at first, and it traced back to patterns in their training data.
